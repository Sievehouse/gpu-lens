# Code of Conduct

GPU Lens is built by people who run GPUs for a living and people who are just getting started. Everyone is welcome
here, and everyone deserves to be treated with respect.

## Our standards

We expect everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space — to:

* be kind and patient, and assume good intent;
* give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not about people;
* respect different experience levels, backgrounds, identities and opinions;
* stay on topic and keep discussions useful to others;
* credit other people's work.

These are not acceptable:

* harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or imagery;
* personal attacks, insults, or trolling;
* publishing someone's private information without permission;
* sexualised language or attention;
* any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the contact options
on the maintainers' GitHub profiles, or, for anything involving a security issue, through GitHub private
vulnerability reporting. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and may respond with a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from project
spaces, depending on the severity and history of the behaviour. Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith
may be removed from the project.
